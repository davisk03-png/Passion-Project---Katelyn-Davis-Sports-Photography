MORE THAN
THE MOMENT.
Sports are made up of more than wins and losses. They’re the split-second decisions, the hard-fought battles, the celebrations, the setbacks, and the people who make every game matter.

My goal is to capture those moments as they happen, authentically, intentionally, and with an eye for the details that might otherwise go unnoticed.

Whether it's under the Friday night lights, on the mat, at the track, or on the field, I’m there to tell the story beyond the final score.
